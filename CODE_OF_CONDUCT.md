# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members of this community pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of who you are. We are
committed to being supportive and welcoming, and to behaving respectfully.

## Our Standards

Be kind, be precise, and assume good faith. Disagreement about code, policy
semantics, or design is expected and welcome; personal attacks are not.
Security-sensitive review (this is an authorization engine) is strict about
evidence: show the request, the policy, and the failure. Critique the
reasoning, not the person.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form
- Dismissing a report because of who the reporter is
- Publishing another person's secrets, tokens, or credentials
- Soliciting or pressuring maintainers for priority outside the process

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, code review, and any
community channel associated with this repository.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcement. Complaints should be
reported to the maintainers privately. A first response is expected within
a week. Outcomes follow a graduated scale: a private warning, a temporary
ban from the repository, or a permanent ban for severe or repeated
violations.
